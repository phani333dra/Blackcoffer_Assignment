Construction Accounts Payable / Payroll Analytics in POWER BI

Project Description for Payroll Analytics in POWER BI

The client wanted to process the real estate construction operations data and needed Payroll Analytics in Power BI for their management decisions.

Designed and developed the following BI Reports:

Scaffold hire and purchase

Framework hire and purchase

Tools and equipment hire and purchase

Total invoice by companies

invoice by companies

Amount payable

Vendor payment history

Influencers of framework

Influencers of scaffold

Influencers of tools and equipment

Forecast, total amount, and balance

Forecast

Description amount balance

Developed data pipeline to load data from the data source to the POWER BI so that the reports can be automatically updated and graphs populated whenever there is a change in the data at the data center.
