2 . On-Page SEO Strategies

Following are the steps that shall be taken for the on page SEO

2.1 Find video keywords

Keywords for YouTube are a bit different from keywords that appear on search engines like Google or Bing.

e.g., if you search for “how to make cake,” you’ll see a ton of video results, and at least one is from YouTube. So Keywords that start with “how to.”

2.2 Rename your video file using a target keyword.

Rename your raw file so that it reflects your title or your focus keyword.eg. Raw fine name-1234.mp4 to how_to_use_hello_bar.mp4

2.3 Optimize your tags

Tagging is one of the best SEO features that YouTube provides.

10 to 12 tags are enough.

LSI Keywords- it determine common synonyms, context and related entities to that of query and the documents to figure out and make your video on top by using similar LSI keywords.

2.4 Optimize your video description.

YouTube video descriptions is 1,000 characters. If you do choose to write a longer description, keep in mind that YouTube only displays the first two or three lines of text — that amounts to about 100 characters.

After that point, viewers have to click “show more” to see the full description.

That’s why we suggest front-loading the description with the most important information, like CTAs or crucial links.

2.5 Tell people to comment

Comments are one of the metrics YouTube uses to decide what people like the most.

It’s a good idea to end your video with a question to get a discussion started.

2.6 Encourage people to subscribe

It’s one of the factors YouTube’s algorithms use to rank videos.

Liking or commenting on a video is a one-time action, but subscribing means that people will see your content on a regular basis

2.7 Step up the production value

Quality of your video must be good, which will in turn increase search performance.

2.8 Make an eye-catching thumbnail

A good thumbnail means more clicks, and that means better SEO.

Make sure your thumbnail stands out and tells viewers what your video is all about.

Many people will view videos just from looking at the thumbnails, so don’t skip this step.

2.9 Add closed captions

People who are deaf or hard of hearing, but they have an unexpected SEO benefit.

2.10 Build links to your channel and your videos too

2.11 Share on social media/Facebook/twitter/LinkedIn.

2.12 Quora is one of the best places to share your videos.

2.13 Send an email to your list

2.14 Embed your video

It gets your video in front of more people-blog posts

It will increase your user engagement. This is a direct result of getting more people to watch your video.

2.15 Start your description with your focus keyword

YouTube’s bot will see your keyword almost immediately and crawl it. That could result in a higher ranking for your keyword.

2.16 Use YouTube for keyword research

Actual phrases that people are searching, so it makes complete sense to use them for your own videos.

Use YouTube for keyword research

2.17 Increase your watch time

If people spend a lot of time watching your videos, they obviously like what you’re doing.

2.18 Review your watch time report

There’s the watch time report, which helps you see how many minutes people are spending on your videos.

2.19 Look at your audience retention report

You should also know how much of your audience actually loves your content enough to stick around for a while.

The audience retention report shows you a handful of helpful metrics, including average view duration and your top performing videos.

2.20 Use engagement reports

It helps you measure your overall performance.

Subscribers report shows you how many subscribers you’ve gained or lost.

Share report tracks how much your videos have been shared using the Share button on YouTube.

These metrics are perfect for improving your YouTube SEO and observing even the finest details of your performance.

2.21 Add some suspense

Start off your video with a bit of suspense.

Don’t create dramatic cliffhangers, but something has to be at stake.

Another option is to ask a question. It doesn’t matter too much how you do it, but you want your viewers to keep watching.

Creating a bit of suspense is one of the best ways to accomplish that.

2.22 Create playlists

Using playlists is one of those small but mighty tactics that can increase view time and retention.

2.23 Get featured on another channel

If you have a YouTube channel about food, find a channel that’s all about beverages. If you have a marketing channel, find a sales channel.

Make sure you get a link to your channel! You can even ask for an annotation.
