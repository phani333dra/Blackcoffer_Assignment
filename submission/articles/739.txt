MACHINE CONNECTION

In the machine connection, all the heavy assets of the petrol station are to be connected. Those connected assets are the reservoir, vending machines, truck carriers, roof sheds, etc. The idea is to improve productivity and reduce costs through predictive maintenance and data insights. In all these, sensors are to be attached to provide real-time data for analysis. Sensors like – environment sensors are also to be attached to know weather conditions, temperature, pressure, etc. To monitor these assets satellite imagery may also be used. There shall be video streaming also to understand the environment.

Future asset problems may be predicted to reduce downtime and efficiency improvement. Dust is one of the important challenges that have to be monitored in a service station. The well-coordinated system has to be maintained among assets for maximum productivity. The only improvement in productively and cost reduction can contribute to a higher margin. The neglected part has to be pointed out and it has to be also managed through our software. All the assets are to be in synchronization for productivity improvement. Any anomaly or defect in machines is to be also pointed out or detected in real-time. Not only among machines or assets of the operator but other operator’s machines and assets may also be connected for a comparative perspective and to understand the competitive advantage.

In the machine connection, we will enable network devices to exchange information and perform actions without the manual assistance of people in the service station. It would be managed by technology with some of the parts of the machine be automated. There shall be a central point for analysis by integrating sensors, the internet, and computers. The asset connection has to be strategic to achieve the purpose of productivity improvement, cost reduction, operation speed, hyper-digital orientation, and to help large scale businesses with accurate prediction or output for decision making. Various new insights are to be drawn to take action for a profitable business. Machine connection will monitor the critical infrastructure of service stations with minimum people intervention. It will also monitor service station traffic in real-time. The sensors are to be of high quality. These sensors can send details about pressure, flow rate, temperature, moisture content, and even fuel levels and inventory. Traffic volume and speed have to be monitored by using control devices like lights and information signs. Using the incoming data, the software will maximize traffic speed and other vehicles operating in a service station. Energy efficiency is another aspect in the service station and it has to be monitored through our software. Our central hub has to dispatch data to the owner or person in- charge’s mobile or wearable in real-time. It will consume less power and here both consumers and suppliers will be benefitted by getting the information.

For our central hub, to identify and to keep the data record, all the incoming vehicle to service station be identified with their vehicle number with an ID, and that may be of use for new business purposes. There shall be also automatic counting of how many two-wheelers, four-wheelers get fuel filled or any other. How many times at a time liquid did flow, that reading will be studied for business transformation and scale purpose. How many smart petrol cards users are there in your area are to be studied. There shall be monitoring and update of digital billboard about service station business activity and other, and on pricing, and events.

Our predictive e-maintenance should provide Optimization, prediction, clustering, classification, benchmarking and so on. The machine synchronization module will connect to other departments of operation speed, business transformation, and business scale. For health management of service station, there shall be a) service station clustering with similar machines for sound comparison, and b) local cluster fault detection to evaluate the similarity of individual machines. Here, the global model may not work but there shall be information sharing among local clusters for better health of service station through our service hub. By leveraging data generated from other similar service stations, the problem or fault detection can be done in a service station and it may have a comparison to the other three departments in a broader sense. Critical model parameters like training samples, components, and alarming limits set are the same for all the units regardless of their different functionalities. But, sometimes false alert discourages the user. However, this peer to peer comparison methodology inside a machine network could improve the fault detection accuracy significantly. Harvesting of values from machines/assets also needs a redesign of the service station. For example, the location may reach both sides of the road despite the facility is in one side.
