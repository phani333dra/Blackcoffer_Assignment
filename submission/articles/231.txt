AI-Powered Sales Support Agent using Retell AI

The Problem

Customer support teams frequently handle repetitive queries related to order tracking, such as:

“What is my order status?”

“What is the transaction ID?”

“When will my order be delivered?”

These queries:

Consume significant agent time

Increase operational costs

Lead to delays during peak hours

Depend heavily on manual lookup in CRM/Excel systems

Our Solution

We developed an AI-powered Sales Support Voice Agent using Retell AI that automates order-related queries.

Customers simply provide their Order ID via voice

The AI agent retrieves: Order Status Transaction ID Expected Delivery Date

Responds instantly with accurate information

The solution eliminates manual intervention and ensures 24/7 availability.
