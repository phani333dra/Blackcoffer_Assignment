Financial Modeling of the Investment Management Professionals

Client Background

Client: A leading financial institution in Europe

Industry Type: Research, Finance

Services: Financial Services

Organization Size: 200+

Challenges

The client was having a huge dataset mostly textual information, and complex information was managed in the database. The dataset was a combination of online responses, texts, and numerical values. The client wanted to

Analyze the response database

Analyze textual database

Generate insights by combining the analysis of response data and the textual data

Visualize and report the outcomes for further data-driven decision-making processes

Solution for Financial Modeling

Blackcoffer provided a decision support framework with the analysis of the response data, textual data and triangulation of the response and the textual datasets. The analytical outcomes was driven by the cutting edge algorithms, financial models and scalable tools.

Blackcoffer developed analytical solutions using the following process:

Descriptive analytics Data cleaning and imputations summary statistics graphics Cross tabulations

Statistical analysis Hypothesis Model creation Model testing Predictive modeling Testing and validations

Analysis of textual data sets, NLP

Triangulation
