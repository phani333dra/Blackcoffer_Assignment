AI Interviewer Agent Using VAPI

The Problem

Recruitment processes today face several challenges:

High manual effort in initial candidate screening

Inconsistent evaluation due to human bias

Limited interviewer availability causing delays

Poor scalability when handling large applicant volumes

Lack of standardized feedback across candidates

These issues lead to increased hiring time, higher costs, and inconsistent hiring quality .

Our Solution

We developed an AI-powered Voice Interviewer using VAPI that:

Conducts automated, real-time interviews

Asks dynamic, role-based questions

Understands candidate responses via speech recognition

Evaluates answers using LLMs

The system acts as a virtual recruiter, capable of interviewing multiple candidates simultaneously with consistent evaluation criteria.
