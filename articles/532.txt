Descriptive vs Inquisitive vs Predictive Analytics

Descriptive Analytics

Use data aggregation and data mining techniques to provide insight into the past and answer: “What has happened?”

Techniques

Data modeling

Trend Reporting

Regression Analysis

Correlations

Inquisitive Analytics

Why did something happen at a certain moment in the past? To study data to validate/reject business hypotheses. This includes

Analytical drill-downs into data

Statistical analysis

Factor analysis

Predictive Analytics

Use statistical models and forecast techniques to understand the future and answer: “What could happen?”

Data modeling

Forecasting

Predictive Modeling
