Chemical engineering AI Tools & AI Software

​Artificial intelligence (AI) is revolutionizing chemical engineering by enhancing process optimization, predictive maintenance, and molecular design. Below is a curated list of AI tools and software commonly utilized in the field:​ Beam AI+3DEV Community+3Valispace+3

1. Molecular Modeling and Simulation:

ACD/Labs: Utilizes AI to predict chemical properties, aiding in understanding chemical behavior under various conditions.​ Chemojo

ChemDraw: Incorporates AI techniques to assist in chemical structure drawing and property prediction.​

ChemAxon: Employs AI for cheminformatics solutions, including chemical property predictions and database management.​

2. Process Simulation and Optimization:

Aspen Plus: Integrates AI to model and optimize chemical processes, enhancing efficiency and reducing costs.​

gPROMS: Uses AI for process modeling and optimization, facilitating advanced process analysis.​

COMSOL Multiphysics: Incorporates AI to simulate and optimize chemical engineering processes across multiple physics domains.​

3. Data Analysis and Machine Learning Platforms:

MATLAB: Offers AI toolboxes for data analysis, modeling, and simulation in chemical engineering applications.​

Python with SciPy and scikit-learn: Provides open-source libraries for machine learning and data analysis, widely used in chemical engineering research.​ GitHub

4. Process Monitoring and Control:

Siemens SIMATIC PCS 7: Utilizes AI for process control systems, improving automation and operational efficiency.​

Honeywell Experion PKS: Incorporates AI to enhance process monitoring and control in chemical plants.​

5. Specialized AI Tools:

Chemist-X: An AI agent that automates reaction condition recommendations in chemical synthesis using retrieval-augmented generation technology. ​ arXiv+1arXiv+1

Distillation Gym: A reinforcement learning environment where AI agents design distillation trains, aiding in process synthesis. ​ arXiv

These tools exemplify the integration of AI in chemical engineering, fostering advancements in process efficiency, safety, and innovation.​
