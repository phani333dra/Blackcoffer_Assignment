Project Description

Create data models, visually represent your survey data with charts and graphs, create reports, and share reports with client.

Our Solution

Step 1: First create sample question in surveymonkey and get some sample response regarding that survey.

Step 2: After that download that response in excel or CSV format

Step 3: Connect that data (Excel or CSV) in Power BI.

Step 4: After that create report using that data in response and format the report properly.
