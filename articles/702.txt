Ikiga Data, a Global Careers Data and Insights Platform

Project Description for Ikiga Data

We built data, insights, and solutions for training providers to measure, manage and market their Returns on Education.

And we put data, guidance, and networks in the hands of students and alumni, helping them manage resilient career lives.

Working in partnership with career advisors, we have been developing Job Fit: a data-driven tool to explore, pilot, and land new jobs or entirely fresh careers.
